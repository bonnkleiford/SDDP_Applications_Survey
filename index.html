Scope and journals

How the corpus was assembled and what counts as a paper in it.

What is included

  • Papers that develop, apply, or use as a benchmark SDDP or a variant (SDDiP, DR-SDDP, and others) on a concrete problem instance.
  • Papers published online by 31 October 2026.
  • Each paper counts once, with one primary domain and application area. Online-first and issue versions are merged.
  • Method studies on academic test problems are listed as Academic / Theory and kept out of the domain shares.
  • Classical decomposition papers and review articles are cited in the survey but not counted.
  • A dagger (†) marks papers whose models include discrete decisions.
